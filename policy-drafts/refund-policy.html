Right of withdrawal (14 days)

If you are a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days, without giving any reason. The 14 days start on the day after you (or a third party you named, other than the carrier) receive the goods. If your order arrives in several deliveries, the period starts the day after you receive the last item.

How to withdraw

You can withdraw online, without an account, at nexaathletics.store/pages/withdraw (button: "Withdraw from contract here"). You can also tell us clearly that you want to withdraw, for example by email to kevin.mlocek2007@gmail.com or by phone on 06 23339806, with your order number and name. You may use the model withdrawal form below, but you do not have to. Send your notice before the 14 days end.

Returning the goods

Send the goods back without undue delay and within 14 days after you told us you are withdrawing. Please return them unused and in their original condition where possible. You bear the direct cost of returning the goods. Contact us first so we can give you the correct return address: [return address and procedure — to be confirmed once fulfillment is set up].

You are only liable for any loss in value of the goods resulting from handling beyond what is needed to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.

Refund

We refund the price you paid and the standard delivery costs within 14 days after we receive your withdrawal notice. We may wait until we have received the goods back, or until you show proof that you sent them back, whichever is earlier. We refund using the same payment method you used for the order, at no extra charge to you. If you chose a more expensive delivery option than our standard one, the extra cost is not refunded.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If you receive an item that is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and a photo. Your legal rights of conformity (warranty) under Dutch and EU law are not affected by this policy.
