# Interim acknowledgement of a withdrawal (manual reply)

Draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. Use ONLY until the acknowledgement is automated (see STORE_LOG.md).

The acknowledgement has to reach the consumer promptly, by email (a durable medium), and contain the
content of their withdrawal statement plus the date and time they submitted it. Reply to the Shopify
notification email ("New customer message on ..."); check that the reply goes to the customer's address
before sending. Take the date/time from the "Submitted at" line or the email's timestamp.

Tip: make a Gmail filter on the words "Withdrawal from contract" so these emails are starred, labelled
and never land in spam.

Missing facts are left in [brackets] on purpose; fill them in once they exist.

Subject: We received your withdrawal - order {order number}

Hello {name},

We confirm that we received your withdrawal statement on {date and time, with time zone}.
